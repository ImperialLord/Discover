What is Discover?

A knowledge OS
not just a dashboard

Every discovery, proof, simulation, and relationship is a first-class object — tracked, evidenced, and connected. Discover runs locally, syncs to the cloud, and keeps a permanent, timestamped record of everything a team builds.

🗄️
Local-first, cloud-synced
evolve_server.py runs on your machine. Render hosts the public layer.
📐
Evidence-graded objects
Every Atlas object carries a label: Known → Supported → Possible → Speculative → Rejected.
🔗
Typed relationships
Objects connect via routed_to, sourced_from, relates_to, proofed_by, recorded_in.
👥
Multi-builder
Real-time presence, sessions, passports, and activity logs for every collaborator.
